1. **Expensive Subscriptions**: Paying **$29 / month (Pro) to $79 / month (Team)** drains your budget year after year for simple utility, coding, or automation tasks.
2. **Proprietary & Cloud-Dependent**: Strict monthly task credits that run out during long multi-step workflows, cloud-rendered browser latency, cannot interact with native desktop Windows software.

4. **Data Privacy & Telemetry Risks**: Your confidential code, text snippets, voice recordings, and business metadata are frequently processed on remote cloud servers outside of your control.
