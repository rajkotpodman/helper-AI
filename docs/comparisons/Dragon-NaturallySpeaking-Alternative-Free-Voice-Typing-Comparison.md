## 🛑 The Hidden Problems with Dragon NaturallySpeaking

1. **Expensive Subscriptions**: Paying **$500+ One-Time / $150 Annual Upgrades** drains your budget year after year for simple utility or automation tasks.
2. **Proprietary & Cloud-Dependent**: Exorbitant enterprise price tag ($500+), massive gigabyte installation footprint, sluggish legacy codebase, locked to single-machine license keys.
3. **Limited Functional Scope**: Most commercial tools only do one narrow job (such as typing text or remote viewing). They cannot autonomously plan, execute terminal commands, crawl the web, or fix computer errors.
4. **Data Privacy Risks**: Your audio, text snippets, and computer metadata are frequently processed on remote cloud servers outside of your control.
