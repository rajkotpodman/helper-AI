## 🛑 The Hidden Problems with Superwhisper Pro

1. **Expensive Subscriptions**: Paying **$8.49 / month ($149 Lifetime)** drains your budget year after year for simple utility or automation tasks.
2. **Proprietary & Cloud-Dependent**: High lifetime price tag ($149), heavy GPU resource consumption on older PCs, macOS-first with incomplete Windows support, no autonomous tool execution.
3. **Limited Functional Scope**: Most commercial tools only do one narrow job (such as typing text or remote viewing). They cannot autonomously plan, execute terminal commands, crawl the web, or fix computer errors.
4. **Data Privacy Risks**: Your audio, text snippets, and computer metadata are frequently processed on remote cloud servers outside of your control.
